COMMENTS:
The architectural SDD is strong and generally follows the expected structure
for an architectural-level design document.  The design presents a coherent
web-based architecture, clearly identifies major responsibilities, provides
architectural reasoning, and establishes boundaries between user interface,
integration, ingestion, AI processing, and persistence concerns.

The document's strongest qualities are its explicit CSC decomposition, clear
interaction descriptions, strong treatment of external integrations, and good
traceability back to the requirements.  The most significant opportunities
for improvement are stronger coverage of performance requirements, more
complete discussion of operational concerns, and reducing the amount of
implementation-specific version information.

The document demonstrates sound architectural thinking, strong decomposition
of responsibilities, clear interfaces, useful design rationale, and
excellent consistency with the requirements.  The CSC structure significantly
improves the clarity of the original requirements architecture while
preserving traceability.

The strongest sections are the major software components, Brightspace
integration architecture, software interactions, security boundaries, and
architectural diagrams.

Overall Rating: Strong Architectural SDD

Very well done, group!  Keep it up!
