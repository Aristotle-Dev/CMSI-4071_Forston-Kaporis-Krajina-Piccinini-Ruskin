Software Development Plan [SDP]
CMSI 4071 Fall 2026
=======================================

INTRODUCTION/DELIVERABLES SECTION:
   Assessment: Very Good
      Strengths
         • Includes major project milestones from proposal through
           final delivery.
         • Deliverables are assigned target completion dates.
         • Software engineering documents such as requirements, SDP,
           architecture, and detailed design are included.
         • Demonstration, testing, documentation, and final delivery
           activities are incorporated.
         • Deliverable descriptions clearly explain their intended
           purpose.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Acceptance criteria for deliverables are not defined.
         • Deliverable ownership is not explicitly assigned.
      Suggestions
         1. Define measurable acceptance criteria for each major
            deliverable, including required reviews, testing, and
            approval activities.
         2. Assign a primary owner and reviewer for each deliverable to
            improve accountability and progress tracking.

PROJECT RESOURCES SECTION:
   Assessment: Excellent
      Strengths
         • Hardware and software resources are clearly separated.
         • Software resources are comprehensive and well documented.
         • Technologies are linked directly to planned project
           functionality.
         • Development, testing, deployment, AI processing, and
           integration tools are identified.
         • Resource descriptions explain why each tool is needed.
         • Multiple testing and debugging resources are included.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Hardware resources are described only at a high level.
         • Capacity planning and resource contingency information are
           not discussed.
      Suggestions
         1. Specify minimum hardware requirements for development,
            testing, and deployment environments.
         2. Include contingency plans for critical third-party
            services such as Firebase, Brightspace APIs, and LLM
            providers.

PROJECT ORGANIZATION SECTION:
   Assessment: Good
      Strengths
         • Team roles are clearly identified.
         • Leadership and primary technical responsibilities are
           assigned.
         • Frontend and backend ownership are separated logically.
         • Team structure is easy to understand.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Responsibilities are described very briefly.
         • No organizational chart is provided.
         • Review responsibilities are not identified.
         • Communication and decision-making processes are not
           discussed.
      Suggestions
         1. Expand role descriptions to include specific development,
            testing, documentation, and review responsibilities.
         2. Add a simple organizational chart and define meeting,
            communication, and decision-making procedures.

PROJECT SCHEDULE SECTION:
   Assessment: Very Good
      Strengths
         • Includes a PERT chart and Task/Resource Table.
         • Development activities are decomposed into detailed tasks.
         • Tasks cover design, implementation, integration, testing,
           optimization, and documentation.
         • Schedule reflects realistic subsystem development.
         • Resource assignments are connected to implementation tasks.
         • Testing and performance validation tasks are included.
      Areas for Improvement
         • Task durations are not clearly identified.
         • Milestone completion criteria are not specified.
         • Critical-path activities are not explicitly identified.
         • Schedule dependencies could be clearer.
      Suggestions
         1. Add estimated durations and completion criteria for major
            tasks and milestones.
         2. Identify critical-path activities and explicitly document
            task dependencies to improve schedule management.
